💳 Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

Objective: Provide timely credit support to farmers for agricultural and allied activities, including post-harvest expenses.

📘 Overview:

The KCC scheme aims to ensure farmers have easy access to low-interest credit to meet their financial needs for crop production, allied activities (like dairy and fisheries), and personal consumption during the farming season. Introduced in 1998, and later expanded to include animal husbandry and fisheries.

✅ Eligibility:

  • All farmers including tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and oral lessees.
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) involved in farming.
  • Dairy farmers and fishermen are also eligible under the expanded scheme.

📋 Step-by-Step Application Process:

  1. Visit your nearest bank branch (Public, Private, or Cooperative Bank).
  2. Fill out the KCC Application Form (also available on bank websites).
  3. Attach required documents and submit.
  4. Bank verifies documents, assesses credit limit, and issues KCC.
  5. You receive a KCC passbook or RuPay ATM card linked to your account.

📄 Required Documents:

  • Aadhaar Card & PAN Card
  • Land ownership proof or tenancy agreement
  • Photographs
  • Bank account details

💸 Key Benefits:

  • Loans up to ₹3 lakh at an interest rate of 4% with timely repayment.
  • No collateral needed for loans up to ₹1.6 lakh.
  • Flexible withdrawal using RuPay debit card.
  • Funds usable for crop inputs, post-harvest needs, consumption, and allied activities.

⚠️ Tips & Support:

  • Repay loans on time to continue receiving the 3% interest subvention.
  • Apply during the cropping season for timely disbursement.
  • Track your card status at the issuing bank or through customer care.
Farmers can approach Common Service Centers (CSCs) or call PM-Kisan Helpline: 155261 for KCC-related assistance.
