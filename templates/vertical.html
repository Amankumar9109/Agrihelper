Vertical Farming

Grow crops in stacked layers using minimal land and controlled environments.

What is Vertical Farming?

Vertical farming is the practice of growing crops in stacked layers or vertical spaces. It can be done in urban settings using controlled environments such as hydroponics and aeroponics to optimize crop production.

Benefits:

  • Efficient land use
  • Reduced transportation costs (locally grown food)
  • Better control over the growing environment
  • Faster crop turnaround

How It Works:

Vertical farms utilize artificial lighting and climate control systems to optimize growing conditions. Crops are stacked in layers, and different systems like hydroponics are used to deliver water and nutrients.
