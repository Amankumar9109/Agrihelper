Drip Irrigation

Efficient water management method that reduces wastage and improves crop yields.

What is Drip Irrigation?

Drip irrigation is a water-saving agricultural technique that delivers water directly to the roots of plants using a network of tubing, valves, and emitters. It minimizes water wastage and ensures consistent moisture, promoting healthier crops.

Benefits:

  • Efficient use of water resources
  • Improved crop yield
  • Reduces weed growth
  • Prevents soil erosion

How It Works:

The system delivers a small but steady stream of water directly to plant roots, minimizing evaporation and runoff. The controlled application of water helps in better soil moisture retention, increasing agricultural productivity.
