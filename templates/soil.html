Soil Health Monitoring

Using sensors and apps to track nutrients and pH levels for better planting decisions.

What is Soil Health Monitoring?

Soil health monitoring involves using sensors and technology to assess soil quality, including nutrients, pH levels, and moisture content. With this data, farmers can make more informed decisions to improve crop production.

Benefits:

  • Precise control over soil quality
  • Improved crop yields
  • Reduced environmental impact
  • Enhanced sustainable farming practices

How It Works:

By installing sensors in the soil, farmers can measure key parameters such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and moisture levels. Apps connected to these sensors analyze the data and recommend actions for optimal crop growth.
