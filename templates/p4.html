🌐 eNAM - National Agriculture Market

Objective: To create a unified national market for agricultural commodities by integrating the existing APMC mandis through an online trading platform.

📘 Overview:

eNAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market) is a pan-India electronic trading portal launched in 2016 by the Ministry of Agriculture. It connects APMC mandis across states and enables farmers to sell their produce directly to buyers across the country at competitive prices.

✅ Key Features:

  • Real-time price discovery and transparency in trade.
  • Online payment to farmers.
  • Quality testing and grading facility available at mandis.
  • Eliminates middlemen, ensuring better prices for farmers.
  • Currently connected to over 1000 mandis across India.

📋 Step-by-Step Procedure to Register:

  1. Visit the nearest eNAM-enabled mandi or https://enam.gov.in/.
  2. Click on “Farmer Registration” and fill out the form with basic details.
  3. Submit proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN) and land ownership proof.
  4. Receive login credentials for online trading portal or mobile app.
  5. Start selling produce online directly to buyers across India.

📱 Mobile App Access:

Farmers can download the eNAM mobile app to check prices, see buyers' demands, and manage sales on the go.

📈 Benefits of eNAM:

  • Ensures fair and transparent pricing for crops.
  • Reduces dependency on local middlemen or traders.
  • Nationwide access to buyers and sellers.
  • Efficient logistics, faster payment, and grading facilities.

📄 Documents Required:

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details for payment
  • Land records or farmer ID
  • Mobile number for communication and OTP
Farmers can also get help through eNAM toll-free helpline: 1800-270-0224
