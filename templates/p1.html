🌾 PM-KISAN Scheme (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Objective: Provide financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year to small and marginal farmers in 3 equal installments directly into their bank accounts.

📘 Overview:

PM-KISAN is a government scheme that ensures small farmers get timely financial help to meet expenses related to agriculture and allied activities. The funds are directly credited to the farmer's bank account without any middlemen.

👨‍🌾 Who Can Apply?

  • Farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land.
  • Farmers whose names are recorded in land records as of a particular cut-off date.
  • Tenants or sharecroppers are not eligible unless they own land.
Note: Institutional landholders, government employees, and income taxpayers are not eligible.

📋 Step-by-Step Procedure to Apply:

  1. Visit the nearest Revenue/Agriculture Office with the required documents.
  2. Submit the PM-KISAN registration form or apply online at pmkisan.gov.in.
  3. Enter your Aadhaar number, land details, and bank account information.
  4. Upload supporting documents and submit the form.
  5. Track your application using your Aadhaar number or mobile number.

🧾 Required Documents:

  • Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
  • Bank Account Details (with IFSC code)
  • Landholding Documents (Khasra-Khatauni, land receipt)
  • Mobile Number (linked with Aadhaar preferred)

💸 How Will Farmers Receive Money?

After successful verification of your details, you will receive ₹2,000 every 4 months (April–July, August–November, December–March) via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into your linked bank account.

You can check the status of your installment by visiting the PM-KISAN portal and clicking on "Beneficiary Status."

⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions:

  • Incorrect Aadhaar: Visit the Aadhaar center to update it and re-verify at the Agriculture office.
  • Payment Not Received: Check the bank details entered or visit the PM-KISAN helpline: 155261 / 011-24300606
  • Pending Application: Track status on the website or contact the local nodal officer.
