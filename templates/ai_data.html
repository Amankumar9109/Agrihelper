AI & Data Analytics

Make smarter decisions on crop rotation, pest control, and yield prediction using data.

What is AI & Data Analytics?

AI & Data Analytics in agriculture involve using machine learning algorithms and large datasets to make informed decisions about farming practices. This technology helps predict crop yields, identify pests, and optimize irrigation systems.

Benefits:

  • Improved crop management
  • Enhanced yield prediction
  • Efficient resource usage
  • Better pest management

How It Works:

AI analyzes data from sensors, drones, and satellites to provide insights into crop health and environmental conditions. The system uses this data to offer recommendations for crop rotation, pest control, and other farming activities.
