Hydroponics

Growing plants without soil using nutrient-rich water solutions in greenhouses.

What is Hydroponics?

Hydroponics is a method of growing plants without soil, using a water-based solution containing all the essential nutrients. This technique allows for faster growth rates, higher yields, and uses less water than traditional farming methods.

Benefits:

  • Water efficiency
  • Faster crop growth
  • Space efficiency
  • Less pesticide use

How It Works:

Plants are grown in containers filled with water and a nutrient-rich solution. The roots are submerged in the solution, which provides all the necessary nutrients without the need for soil. This method is often used in greenhouses.
