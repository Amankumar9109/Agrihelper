🧪 Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme

Objective: Promote soil test-based and balanced use of fertilizers to improve soil health and crop productivity.

📘 Overview:

Launched in 2015, the Soil Health Card Scheme provides farmers with a detailed report on the nutrient status of their soil. This helps them make informed decisions about crop choice and fertilizer usage, improving yield and reducing cost.

✅ Eligibility:

  • All farmers (small, marginal, and large) are eligible.
  • Farmers owning or cultivating agricultural land.
  • Government collects soil samples every 2 years.

📋 Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Soil sample is collected by trained officials from the farmer’s field.
  2. Sample is tested in authorized Soil Testing Labs.
  3. Analysis is done for 12 key parameters like pH, N, P, K, micronutrients.
  4. Based on the report, a Soil Health Card is generated for each farm.
  5. The card is distributed to the farmer and also available online.

📄 Key Parameters Tested:

  • Macronutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K)
  • Micronutrients: Zinc, Iron, Copper, Manganese
  • pH, Organic Carbon, Electrical Conductivity, Sulphur

📈 Benefits of SHC:

  • Improves crop productivity and yield.
  • Reduces excessive use of chemical fertilizers.
  • Promotes sustainable farming and soil conservation.
  • Guides farmers on appropriate crop choices and treatment.

📌 Where to Apply / Get SHC:

  • Nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Agriculture Office.
  • Online: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/
  • State-level Agriculture Portals or CSCs.
Farmers can request soil testing at any time via local agricultural officers or CSCs. The SHC is valid for two years.
