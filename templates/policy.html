🌱 Government Schemes for Farmers

Explore all central government schemes designed to empower farmers with financial support, insurance, and market access.

👨‍🌾 PM-KISAN

Support: ₹6,000/year to eligible farmers in 3 installments.

How to Apply:

  1. Visit the local revenue or agriculture office.
  2. Submit Aadhaar, land records, and bank passbook.
  3. Register online via pmkisan.gov.in.

🌾 PMFBY

Support: Crop insurance against natural calamities.

How to Apply:

  1. Check if your crop is covered on pmfby.gov.in.
  2. Apply through your bank, CSC, or the PMFBY portal.
  3. Pay a small premium. The government covers the rest.

🧪 Soil Health Card Scheme

Support: Detailed report on soil quality + fertilizer suggestions.

How to Apply:

  1. Contact your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
  2. Give a sample of your farm soil.
  3. Receive a card with suggestions within a few weeks.

📈 eNAM (National Agriculture Market)

Support: Sell crops online at competitive prices across India.

How to Join:

  1. Check if your mandi is registered with eNAM.
  2. Get your produce quality graded.
  3. Register through the mandi or at enam.gov.in.

💳 Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Support: Instant credit for crop input, harvesting, etc.

How to Apply:

  1. Visit any bank with land proof and Aadhaar.
  2. Fill out the KCC form or apply through the PM-KISAN portal.
  3. Get the card in a few days to use like a debit card.
