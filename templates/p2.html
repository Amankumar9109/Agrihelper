🌾 Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Objective: Provide affordable crop insurance to farmers and ensure financial support against crop failure due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

📘 Overview:

PMFBY is a government-backed crop insurance scheme that protects farmers against crop loss and ensures risk coverage during sowing to harvesting. The scheme promotes stability in farm income and encourages modern farming practices.

✅ Eligibility:

  • All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas.
  • Loanee and non-loanee farmers are eligible.
  • Tenant and sharecropper farmers can apply with land documentation.

📋 Step-by-Step Application Process:

  1. Visit a nearby CSC, bank, or agriculture office.
  2. Fill out the PMFBY application form or apply online at pmfby.gov.in.
  3. Select your crop, season, and provide land & personal details.
  4. Upload required documents and pay the premium.
  5. Get acknowledgment with a reference number for claim tracking.

🧾 Required Documents:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Landholding Certificate or Land Record
  • Bank Passbook with IFSC code
  • Photograph (passport-sized)

💸 Benefits:

  • Risk coverage against natural calamities like floods, droughts, cyclones.
  • Low premium rates for Kharif (2%), Rabi (1.5%), and commercial crops (5%).
  • Post-harvest loss and localized event coverage included.
  • Claim amount is directly transferred to the farmer's bank account via DBT.

⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions:

  • Claim not received: Ensure correct bank/Aadhaar details. Contact helpline 1800-180-1111.
  • Document rejection: Visit local agriculture office or CSC for re-verification.
  • Application pending: Track status on official website.
Tip: Apply before the sowing season deadline to be eligible. Always collect acknowledgment and keep reference ID for future claims.
