# MODERN FARMING TECHNIQUES & PRECISION AGRICULTURE

## Precision Drip Irrigation (Micro-Irrigation)
Drip irrigation delivers water and nutrients directly to the root zone of plants through a network of valves, pipes, tubing, and emitters.
- Water Savings: Saves 40% to 70% of water compared to traditional flood irrigation.
- Yield Improvements: Increases crop productivity by 20% to 90% depending on crop type.
- Fertigation Capability: Fertilizers can be dissolved directly into the irrigation water, reducing nutrient waste and runoff.
- Best suited crops: Sugarcane, cotton, banana, grapes, pomegranate, tomato, capsicum, potato, and orchard crops.
- Subsidies in India: Under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - 'Per Drop More Crop', small and marginal farmers receive up to 55% financial assistance/subsidy, and other farmers receive 45% subsidy on drip system installations.
- Maintenance: Regular flushing of sub-mains and lateral lines once a month prevents clogging caused by silt, algae, and salt precipitation. Acid treatment using diluted hydrochloric or phosphoric acid dissolves mineral deposits.

## Soil Health & Nutrient Management
Healthy soil is the foundation of high-yield sustainable agriculture.
- Ideal Soil pH: Most agricultural crops thrive in soil pH between 6.0 and 7.5. Acidic soils (pH < 6.0) require agricultural lime (calcium carbonate) or dolomite, while alkaline/saline soils (pH > 8.0) benefit from gypsum application and organic green manuring.
- NPK Balance: Nitrogen (N) stimulates leafy vegetative growth, Phosphorus (P) promotes strong root establishment and flowering, and Potassium (K) enhances disease resistance, drought tolerance, and grain filling. Standard general guideline is 4:2:1 for cereals, but precise ratio must follow the Soil Health Card recommendations.
- Micronutrient Deficiencies: Zinc deficiency causes 'Khaira' disease in rice, iron deficiency leads to chlorosis in young leaves, and boron deficiency causes hollow heart in cauliflower and poor grain setting in wheat.
- Soil Organic Carbon (SOC): Indian soils generally have low SOC (<0.5%). Raising SOC to above 0.75% through farmyard manure (FYM), vermicompost, and crop residue incorporation dramatically improves water retention and microbial activity.

## Vertical Farming & Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA)
Vertical farming cultivates crops in vertically stacked layers in controlled indoor environments without natural sunlight or soil.
- Space Efficiency: Produces 10x to 20x higher yield per square meter compared to traditional open-field farming.
- Artificial Lighting: Uses customized LED light recipes with red (660nm) and blue (450nm) spectrums to optimize photosynthesis.
- Climatic Control: Temperature (20°C–25°C), relative humidity (60%–70%), and CO2 concentration (800–1200 ppm) are continuously regulated.
- Ideal Crops: High-value microgreens, lettuce, spinach, kale, strawberries, herbs (basil, mint), and exotic mushrooms.
- High initial capital expenditure (CAPEX) is required, but it offers year-round harvest independent of external weather risks.

## Agricultural Drones & Remote Sensing
Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) transform crop monitoring and agrochemical application.
- Precision Spraying: Agricultural spray drones equipped with 10L–20L tanks can cover 1 acre in 6–10 minutes with ultra-low volume (ULV) nozzles, saving 90% water and 25% pesticide while protecting farmers from toxic chemical exposure.
- Multispectral & NDVI Mapping: Drones equipped with multispectral sensors measure the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) to detect crop stress, pest infestations, nutrient deficiencies, and moisture stress up to two weeks before visual symptoms appear.
- Indian Government Initiatives: The 'Kisan Drone Scheme' provides 40% to 100% subsidies to Agricultural Universities, FPOs, and rural youth entrepreneurs through the Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM).

## Hydroponics & Soilless Cultivation
Hydroponics is the technique of growing plants in mineral nutrient solutions in water without soil.
- Common Systems: Nutrient Film Technique (NFT), Deep Water Culture (DWC), Drip Hydroponics, and Aeroponics.
- Growth Substrates: Inert media such as coco peat (coir pith), perlite, vermiculite, and rockwool provide root support.
- Water Efficiency: Recirculating closed-loop hydroponic systems use up to 90% less water than open field cultivation.
- EC & pH Control: Electrical Conductivity (EC) must be maintained between 1.2 and 2.5 mS/cm and pH between 5.5 and 6.5 for optimal nutrient absorption.

## AI & Data Analytics in Smart Farming
Artificial intelligence combined with IoT sensors optimizes agricultural decision-making.
- Predictive Analytics: Machine learning algorithms analyze historical weather patterns, soil moisture data, and satellite imagery to recommend exact sowing dates and harvest windows.
- Computer Vision for Disease Detection: Mobile camera-based AI models identify crop pests, fungal spots, and leaf blight with over 90% accuracy, suggesting targeted biological and chemical remedies.
- Yield Forecasting: Combines crop growth simulation models with remote sensing to predict harvest tonnage and optimize mandi supply logistics.
